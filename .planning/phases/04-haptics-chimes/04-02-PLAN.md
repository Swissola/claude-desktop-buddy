  <action>Pause for human verification.   <what-built>The StickS3 now emits square-wave chiptune chimes for all five events, a board-conditional "chime" settings label, and a persisted speaker-volume cycler. Approve = E5 (659Hz) bright rising; deny = A3 (220Hz) low; attention = E4-E4-G5; celebrate = C5-E5-G5-C6 arpeggio; connect = G4-C5.</what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    1. Trigger each event on the device and listen: approve, deny, attention, celebrate, connect. Confirm all five are audibly distinct and recognisable.
    2. Specifically confirm approve vs deny are unmistakably different (the thing the motor could not do — D-03).
    3. Confirm deny (A3 220Hz) is clearly audible through the speaker. Small speakers roll off at low frequencies — if deny is weak/inaudible, say so: the documented fallback is to raise CHIME_DENY_FREQ to A4 440Hz (still clearly distinct from approve E5) and re-flash.
    4. Open Settings → cycle the "volume" entry through its levels (mute/low/med/high/max) and confirm loudness changes; reboot and confirm the chosen level persisted.
    5. Press UI buttons while an event chime is playing and confirm the button beep does not clash/stack with the chime (D-06).
  </how-to-verify>
  <verify>

  </verify>
